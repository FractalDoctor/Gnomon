GNOMON

A sundial designer for Mac
HORIZONTAL · VERTICAL · DECLINING · RECLINING · POLAR · EQUATORIAL · ANALEMMATIC

Gnomon designs real sundials, the kind you make and set up in a garden, on a wall or across a patio. Give it the place and the surface and it works out every hour line exactly for that spot, then gives you a full-size template to print and the files to cut, engrave or 3D print it.

A sundial is only as good as its geometry. The hour lines of a dial in Cornwall are not those of one in Edinburgh, and a wall that faces a little west of south needs a different face again. Gnomon does that geometry for you, by exact calculation, for the very place the dial will stand.

Seven Kinds of Dial

  • Horizontal — the classic garden dial on a pedestal
  • Vertical — on a wall facing the equator
  • Declining — on a wall facing any other way
  • Reclining — for a face that leans back, from a sloping roof to a tilted stone
  • Polar — with parallel hour lines
  • Equatorial — both faces drawn, one for summer and one for winter
  • Analemmatic — a lawn or paving dial where you are the gnomon

Exact for Your Spot

  • Find your site by name, use your Mac’s location, or type the latitude and longitude
  • Longitude correction, so the dial keeps standard time for your zone
  • The equation of time as a table, a correction graph, or a figure of eight on each hour line
  • Summer-time numerals beside the standard ones
  • Date lines for the solstices and equinoxes, the signs of the zodiac or the start of each month
  • A thick gnomon handled properly, with the morning and afternoon lines drawn from its two edges

Find Which Way Your Wall Faces

A wall dial is only right if you know exactly which way the wall faces. Gnomon finds it three ways: draw along the wall on a satellite map, time the shadow of a plumb line on a level board, or take a compass bearing and correct it to true north.

See It in Place Before You Make It

A live 3D preview stands your dial in a garden, on a wall or over a lawn, under the real Sun for any date and time. Drag the time through the day and watch the shadow cross the hours. Play a whole year and watch the shadow of the nodus trace its figure of eight. Frame the dial with the Sun in view, or wait for night and see the stars turn overhead.

Print It, Cut It, Make It

  • A full-size template tiled across A4, A3, Letter and larger paper, with overlaps, registration marks and a 100 mm check bar on every sheet
  • A one-page proof to check the layout first
  • SVG for laser cutters and engravers, in separate layers to cut, engrave and score, with the lettering as outlines
  • Full-size PDF, PNG, and CSV tables of every angle
  • 3MF and STL for 3D printing, and GLB, OBJ, USD and USDZ models
  • Open formats throughout, so the files work in the tools you already have

Ten Period Styles

Plain, Art Deco, Retro, Futuristic, Anglo-Futurist, Ship’s Brass, Alpine Fresco, Medieval Mass Dial, Victorian Slate and Imperial Chinese. Each sets its own lettering, ornament, gnomon shape and materials, and none of them ever moves a line. Choose a traditional Latin motto or write your own.

Made for Makers

For woodworkers, stonemasons, metalworkers, gardeners and teachers. Every dial is a document you can save, reopen and change, and the geometry is worked out afresh each time. The lettering uses open-licence typefaces that travel with the files you export.

Privacy

No ads. No tracking. No analytics. No subscriptions. No account.

Every line, every shadow and every position of the Sun is computed on your Mac. Gnomon goes online only when you ask it to find a place by name, name your Mac’s location, or show the satellite map, and each of those is optional. Full detail in the privacy policy.

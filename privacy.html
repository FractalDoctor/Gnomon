GNOMON — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 2026

Data Collection

Gnomon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, registration, or sign-in of any kind.

Network Requests

Gnomon goes online only when you ask it to, and only for three things, each handled by Apple’s own Maps services.

If you search for a place by name, the app hands the text you typed to Apple’s place search to find its coordinates and time zone. If you use your Mac’s location, it asks the same service for the name and time zone of those coordinates. If you open the satellite map to find which way a wall faces, the map shows the area around your dial’s site, which Apple Maps loads in the usual way. Nothing else about you accompanies any of these requests.

You can avoid all three. Typing the latitude and longitude and choosing the time zone from the list needs no network at all, and the wall’s direction can be found from a timed shadow or a compass bearing instead. Every other part of the app — every hour line, date line and correction, every position of the Sun, the 3D preview, the templates and every exported file — is worked out on your Mac.

The compass tool includes a link to the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration’s magnetic declination calculator. It opens in your web browser only if you click it.

Location

If you allow it, Gnomon reads your Mac’s location once, when you click Use This Mac’s Location, to set the site of the dial you are designing. It is never asked for at launch. The coordinates are rounded to two decimal places, about a kilometre, and saved in your dial’s document. You can decline the permission and search for a place or type the coordinates instead; the app works normally either way.

Your Documents

Each dial is a document saved wherever you choose, as with any Mac app, including iCloud Drive if you choose to save it there. It holds the dial’s design and the site you gave it. Gnomon does not upload it anywhere.

Local Settings

Your preferences, such as units, paper size, default style and the folder exports open in, are stored on your Mac using the standard macOS mechanisms. They are not sent anywhere.

Exports, Printing and the Clipboard

Exported files, printed templates and tables copied to the clipboard are produced on your Mac and go only where you send them. Gnomon does not upload them anywhere.

Analytics and Tracking

Gnomon does not include any analytics frameworks, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind. We do not know who you are, where you are, or how you use the app. That is by design.

Third-Party Services

Apart from Apple’s Maps services described above, Gnomon does not communicate with any third-party services. There are no social media integrations, no advertising networks, and no data brokers. The typefaces it letters dials with are bundled inside the app and are never downloaded.

Children’s Privacy

Gnomon does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date. Given that the app collects no data, changes are unlikely.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the Gnomon GitHub repository.
